# **1\. Executive Summary**

This report confirms delivery of the Progressive Web App (PWA) built for ShoSoccer Football Coaching Limited under the Progressive Web App Development Agreement dated 20 February 2026\. In accordance with Section 3.3 and Section 6.1 of the Agreement, delivery of source code, repository access, documentation, and the Client's licence to use its customised platform instance is therefore due.

The 90-day bug-fix warranty under Section 5.1 is active and runs to 30 September 2026\. No outstanding defects are recorded against the platform at the time of this report. The remaining obligation under the Agreement is warranty monitoring through to the warranty end date.

Section 3 of this report presents verified commercial performance data covering session volume, customer growth, transaction volume, and return on investment, drawn from platform reporting and transaction exports.

Figures are reported at the level of precision the underlying source supports, and are labelled to distinguish measured outcomes from estimates or projections. Gross transaction volume is reported throughout; this is total charge value before payment fees and refunds, and is not equivalent to profit.

| :---- | :---- | :---- |

# **7\. Ongoing Hosting Costs (Post-Warranty)**

The PWA runs on two third-party infrastructure services: Vercel, which hosts the application itself, and Supabase, which provides the underlying database, procured through the Vercel Marketplace. These are not optional add-ons; the platform cannot run without them. Both are currently being paid for directly by Series Media Limited, to avoid any interruption to the build, and are entirely separate from the £300 per month Platform Management & Operations Retainer, which covers technical support, CRM workflow management, and reporting.

| Service | Function | Recent Monthly Charge (USD) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Vercel | Application hosting | \$24.40 (approx. £18 at time of charge) |
| Supabase (via Vercel Marketplace) | Database | \$30.50 (approx. £23 at time of charge) |

*Both services bill in USD on consumption-based pricing, so the charge will vary month to month with platform usage, and the GBP equivalent will vary with the exchange rate at the time each charge is made. The figures above are quoted in USD as the primary, stable reference, with the GBP amount shown as an approximate equivalent only. These two charges are kept separate deliberately, as they are billed independently and will not always move together.*

Per Section 5.3 of the Agreement, the warranty does not extend to third-party services or hosting environments; Series Media Limited does not warrant their availability, performance, or cost. Responsibility for the Vercel and Supabase charges transfers to ShoSoccer Football Coaching Limited from 30 September 2026, when the 90-day warranty period ends, and is separate from and in addition to the ongoing £300 per month Retainer. Series Media Limited will provide the necessary account access ahead of that date so billing can be transferred without interruption to the live platform.

# **8\. Proposed Developer Retainer (Post-Warranty)**

Once the 90-day warranty period ends on 30 September 2026, Peter Phelan / Thirdwave Software will no longer be obligated to respond to defects under the warranty response times set out in Section 6\. Series Media Limited recommends putting a small monthly retainer in place with the developer, rather than moving to a per-incident or per-project quote basis, to secure priority response and continuity of support should an issue with the app arise.

This is proposed on the basis that a standing retainer, however modest, keeps the original developer engaged and responsive, avoids the delay of sourcing and briefing a new developer from scratch under time pressure, and gives ShoSoccer a defined point of contact who already understands the platform architecture. A per-project quote model, by contrast, carries no guaranteed response time and no priority over the developer's other work.

*Series Media Limited has requested a rate from Peter Phelan for this retainer; the amount and the specific terms it would cover (response time commitment and/or a fixed monthly hours allowance) are to be confirmed and will be provided to ShoSoccer once received. This is put forward as a recommendation for consideration, not as a term already agreed or in effect.*
